# EduMotive Admin

Internal panel for the EduMotive editorial team (sign-in: edumotive4). It manages everything shown on the EduMotive website and its subscription business: writing/editing and approving articles submitted by students and authors, article sections, authors, badges and author tiers, magazine issues (PDF flipbook), programs and results (with website visibility switches), the team and About Us content, subscription plans, payment verification and Speed Post tracking numbers, advertiser submissions, reader letters, and a notes & content-planning board.
